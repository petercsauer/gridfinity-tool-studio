        Drag a point, a handle or the outline. Double-click to add a point or switch corner and smooth.

        Tab steps through the points. Arrow keys move a point 0.1 mm, with Shift 1 mm. D or Delete removes it, A adds a point after it (or on the picked stretch of outline), C switches it between corner and smooth, S straightens the segment after it, R rounds a corner to the radius beside Round corner. Minus gives less detail (fewer points), plus more. Command or Control Z undoes. Hold Option or Alt while dragging to stop straight edges snapping level, or to break a smooth point's handles apart.
