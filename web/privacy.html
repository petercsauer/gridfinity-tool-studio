Privacy

Gridfinity Tool Studio runs entirely in your browser. Your photos are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Your photos and tools

When you upload a photo, finding the calibration sheet, removing the background and tracing the tool all happen on this device. The photo is not sent anywhere.

Each traced tool (a reduced, perspective-corrected image of the sheet, the outline of each part on it, and how you arranged the parts in the bin) is stored only in this browser’s storage, so it is still there next time. Select an uploaded tool and choose Remove to delete it, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings to delete everything. At most 20 uploads are kept; older ones are removed first.

Downloads from other services

The first time you upload a photo, the app asks before it downloads the background-removal AI model (about 88 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). It also loads the onnxruntime engine that runs the model from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Both are cached by your browser. Those services see an ordinary download request (your IP address and browser details), as with any website; they never receive your photos.

Hosting

The app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which serves its files and may keep standard request logs for security and operations.

No tracking

There are no analytics scripts, no advertising, no accounts and no cookies.

To see how many people use it, the app counts a few steps anonymously: choosing a photo, accepting or declining the model download, whether the upload worked (and if not, why: no sheet found, no tool found, tool cut off, photo unreadable, AI model failed to download or run, out of memory, or another error), and exporting an STL, SVG or DXF. Each step only requests an empty file from this site (for example e/upload-ok.txt), which shows up as a number in the host’s request statistics. Nothing about you or your photo is sent with it, and there is no ID that links one step to another.
